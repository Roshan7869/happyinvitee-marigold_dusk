सात फेरे

Seven rounds,
seven promises

1of seven
  1. PHERA ONE

    To provide

    That we will keep a home of plenty, and that neither of us will want for food, health or care while the other has it to give.ॐ इषे एकपदी भव

  2. PHERA TWO

    To grow strong

    That we will keep each other well in body and in mind, and face what comes with our strength pooled rather than counted separately.ॐ ऊर्जे द्विपदी भव

  3. PHERA THREE

    To prosper

    That we will look after what we build together, and spend it as gladly on others as on ourselves.ॐ रायस्पोषाय त्रिपदी भव

  4. PHERA FOUR

    To be happy

    That we will make each other laugh, and that our families will always find the door open and the kettle on.ॐ मायोभवाय चतुष्पदी भव

  5. PHERA FIVE

    To raise

    That we will care for the generations either side of us — those who raised us, and whoever comes after.ॐ प्रजाभ्यः पञ्चपदी भव

  6. PHERA SIX

    To keep faith

    That through every season, the good years and the thin ones, we will stay at each other’s side.ॐ ऋतुभ्यः षट्पदी भव

  7. PHERA SEVEN

    To be friends

    That above husband and wife, above every other thing we promise today, we will remain each other’s closest friend.ॐ सखे सप्तपदी भव
